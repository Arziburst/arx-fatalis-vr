# The scenarios of the test save library (#70), played by scripts\dev\make-test-saves.ps1 in ONE game run
# (flat, or on the simulated headset with -VrSim): a new game from the main menu, then the player is put at
# each scenario point with channel commands and saved there. Format and directives: the header of
# make-test-saves.ps1.
#
# Level 1 (the goblin prison, where a new game starts). Ids and coordinates come from the `entities`
# channel command (docs/vr/TESTING.md); a position is "x y z" in world units (about 100 per metre). `setpos`
# takes the feet: its y is the floor the player stands on, and the eye (`player.y`) ends 170 units higher
# (smaller y; TRAPS.md, #210). An entity's own position is on the floor, and walls are everywhere:
# a spot set with `setpos` is checked with a screenshot (`shot`), never by arithmetic alone. A spot in the
# hand reticles' reach of an entity is found by the game itself with `approach <entity>` (#210).
#
# Save names are folder names: scripts use them as `smoke-test.ps1 -Save <name>`.

# --- mvp-new-game: the demo scenario's first step ---------------------------------------------------------------
# The awakening of a new game is scripted: the hero lies in the start cell and the controls stay blocked
# for about 30-40 s. The save is made when they are free (the hero stands, the tutorial text is up).
newgame
@state GAMEPLAY 120
@until player.controlsBlocked False 120
@wait 2
@expect area 1
@describe New game: the hero stands in the start cell of level 1 after the awakening, controls free, nothing equipped.
shot mvp-new-game
@wait 1
save mvp-new-game

# --- scene-kultar: T1 of #29, one step before the Kultar scene's trigger --------------------------------------------
# After the prison intro the hero stands at MARKER_0112 (8540 3105 8460), yaw 0, facing +z; the zone KULTAR_WAKE (armed by
# the intro) lies 2.5-3.5 m ahead, a strip about 1.5 m deep. This spot is 2.2 m ahead, facing +z: a walk forward starts the
# scene (Kultar at the bars: camera entities, cinemascope, playerlookat, three voiced lines).
setpos 8540 3105 8680 0
@wait 2
@describe Kultar at the bars (#29, T1): the hero one step before the trigger zone KULTAR_WAKE, facing +z; walking forward about half a metre starts the scene (camera entities).
shot scene-kultar
@wait 1
save scene-kultar

# The next scenarios fight and pick things up: a sword in the right hand (demo step 3), and the player
# cannot be killed while the script walks him around (switched off again before every save).
invulnerability on
inventory playeraddfromscene short_sword_0030
# Run in the item's context: the item equips itself on the player (`equip <target>` is the item's command)
short_sword_0030.equip player
@entity short_sword_0030 show=6

# --- mvp-lever, mvp-door, mvp-chest: a lever, a portcullis, a chest in the hand reticles' reach (#210) -------------
# `approach <entity>` finds the spot by number (docs/vr/TESTING.md): a floor with room for the player, the eye within
# the pick's reach and nothing of the level before the entity on the rays from the eye and both hands. Called twice:
# far away an entity is not updated, so the first call can only test the level; the second, from there, its faces.
# lever_0012 opens porticullis_0015 (#403); pulled once its goblin is dead, it starts the 'Kultar freed' scene.
approach lever_0012
@wait 1
approach lever_0012
@wait 2
@print player.x player.y player.z camera.eyeAboveFeet
invulnerability off
@describe Lever: a sword equipped; lever_0012 (opens porticullis_0015) in the hand reticles' reach ahead (#38).
shot mvp-lever
@wait 1
save mvp-lever
invulnerability on

approach porticullis_0015
@wait 1
approach porticullis_0015
@wait 2
@print player.x player.y player.z camera.eyeAboveFeet
invulnerability off
@describe Door: a sword equipped; the closed portcullis porticullis_0015 in the hand reticles' reach ahead (#38).
shot mvp-door
@wait 1
save mvp-door
invulnerability on

approach chest_metal_0052
@wait 1
approach chest_metal_0052
@wait 2
@print player.x player.y player.z camera.eyeAboveFeet
invulnerability off
@describe Chest: a sword equipped; chest_metal_0052 in the hand reticles' reach ahead (#38, open it, take what is inside).
shot mvp-chest
@wait 1
save mvp-chest
