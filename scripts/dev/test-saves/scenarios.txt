# The scenarios of the test save library (#70), played by scripts\dev\make-test-saves.ps1 in ONE flat
# game run: a new game from the main menu, then the player is put at each scenario point with channel
# commands and saved there. Format and directives: the header of make-test-saves.ps1.
#
# Level 1 (the goblin prison, where a new game starts). Ids and coordinates come from the `entities`
# channel command (docs/vr/TESTING.md); a position is "x y z" in world units (about 100 per metre) and y
# is the eye: the floor is 170 higher. An entity's own position is on the floor, and walls are everywhere:
# a spot is found with a screenshot (`shot`), never by arithmetic alone. Levers, chests and doors are not
# here: nothing placed the player where one is under the reticle, see the follow-up task named in #70.
#
# Save names are folder names: scripts use them as `smoke-test.ps1 -Save <name>`.

# --- mvp-new-game: the demo scenario's first step ---------------------------------------------------------------
# The awakening of a new game is scripted: the hero lies in the start cell and the controls stay blocked
# for about 30-40 s. The save is made when they are free (the hero stands, the tutorial text is up).
newgame
@state GAMEPLAY 120
@until player.controlsBlocked False 120
@wait 2
@expect area 1
@describe New game: the hero stands in the start cell of level 1 after the awakening, controls free, nothing equipped.
shot mvp-new-game
@wait 1
savegame mvp-new-game

# The next scenarios fight and pick things up: a sword in the right hand (demo step 3), and the player
# cannot be killed while the script walks him around (switched off again before every save).
invulnerability on
inventory playeraddfromscene short_sword_0030
# Run in the item's context: the item equips itself on the player (`equip <target>` is the item's command)
short_sword_0030.equip player
@entity short_sword_0030 show=6

# --- mvp-items: a key on a table, a note and a form beside it, 2.3 m ahead -----------------------------------
setpos 10700 2935 11400
lookat key_base_0005
@wait 2
invulnerability off
@describe Items: a sword equipped; a table with key_base_0005, note_0004 and blank_form_0001 2.3 m ahead (demo step 3, pick up and drag into the inventory).
shot mvp-items
@wait 1
savegame mvp-items
invulnerability on

# --- mvp-goblin-fight: a goblin 3.5 m ahead in the corridor ---------------------------------------------------------
setpos 11454 2935 10250
lookat goblin_base_0036
@wait 2
invulnerability off
@describe Goblin fight: a sword equipped; goblin_base_0036 3.5 m ahead in the corridor (demo step 5, melee).
shot mvp-goblin-fight
@wait 1
savegame mvp-goblin-fight
